# Strategy deprecation register

Status: P0-03, 2026-10. Owner decision OD-03 (signed 2026-10-03): the IBKR :8000 11-strategy pipeline and LLM arm B stay RUNNING, untouched, as the blended control. Statuses below are **advisory**; nothing is removed from any live signal path, no file moved, and the live removal ticket (P0-07) is not built.

Mechanism: `src/firm/strategies/registry.py` carries a status sidecar (`status()`, `target_status()`, `list_allocatable()`, `require_allocatable()`). `list_strategies()` and `get()` are unchanged.

- **LEGACY_LIVE** (the 11 IBKR-enabled strategies): warn-only. The "target" column is the recorded intent, effective only if P0-07 is executed under OD-03.
- **ARCHIVED / DECOMMISSIONED** (already off everywhere): refused (`StrategyNotAllocatableError`) only in new research and backtest entry points added by later tickets; `allow_archived=True` is for legacy replays. In live and API fallback paths (config omits its `strategies` list) they are skipped with a WARNING. Explicit lists in configs are honoured exactly as before.
- Analyst bucket note: removing multi_factor, sentiment and event_driven would empty the fundamental and sentiment analyst buckets (`runtime.py` `_FUND_STRATEGIES`/`_SENT_STRATEGIES`); this is one reason live removal is deferred.

Evidence for the 11: `docs/standalone_strategy_evaluation_2026_09.json` (all 11 `survives=false`; hedged net OOS Sharpe shown), `docs/ensemble_redundancy_audit_2026_09.md`, `docs/edge_search_verdict_2026_09.md`. Literature: McLean & Pontiff (JF 2016) 58% post-publication decline; Chen & Velikov (JFQA 2023) average anomaly about 4 bps/month after costs; Novy-Marx & Velikov (RFS 2016) on turnover.

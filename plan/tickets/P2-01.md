## Algorithm notes / pseudocode (keep the source plan's formulas exactly where they apply; correct them explicitly if wrong, with reason)
Selection rule (written into the rationale doc before the list): include an ETF if (a) it is the earliest-inception vehicle in its asset-class cell whose median dollar ADV over a fixed early window (its first 3 years after inception, pre-seal) exceeds the floor; ties broken by expense ratio, which the owner supplies (dated, owner-supplied inputs are recorded in the rationale). No current AUM is used: the repo holds no AUM data, the agent cannot fetch any, and today's AUM is survivorship- and performance-correlated, contradicting 'no performance input'. (b) it has >= 5 years of history, (c) ADV above the floor. Cap 25 instruments, minimum 15. Asset-class cells fixed in advance (10 cells above); at most 3 per cell. No return, Sharpe, correlation or drawdown is computed or consulted.

Warm-up rule, pre-registered here: the vol estimator (P3-01) uses a 2520-day long window. Until an instrument has 2520 days, use an expanding mean of the same quantity; an instrument enters the portfolio only after 256 return days. `first_trade_date` (inception + 256 return days) is the portfolio-entry date and is what P3-07 reads for per-episode active-instrument counts; `full_vol_window_date` (inception + 2520 days) records when the full window is available, for reporting only.

Document for each instrument: inception, `first_trade_date`, `full_vol_window_date`, prior exposure flag, asset class, and a one-line inclusion reason.

## Rationale (cite source plan / literature)
Source plan P3-05 and Carver. Several partially correlated forecasts for one instrument are combined with fixed handcrafted weights. A forecast diversification multiplier (FDM) restores the average |forecast| to about 10 after the correlation-driven shrinkage. Fixed weights, not Sharpe-maximising weights, are the source's anti-overfitting rule ("DO NOT fit weights by maximising Sharpe").

## Algorithm notes / pseudocode (keep the source plan's formulas exactly where they apply; correct them explicitly if wrong, with reason)
Source formulas, kept: `combined = sum_r w_r * f_r * FDM`, capped +/-20; `FDM = 1 / sqrt(w' rho w)`; rho is the pooled forecast correlation floored at 0; FDM capped at 2.5; handcrafted fixed weights.

Clarifications:
- "Floored at 0" is applied elementwise to the off-diagonal entries (negative correlations are treated as zero, which is conservative, giving a smaller FDM). The diagonal stays 1. This is the usual Carver reading, stated explicitly.
- `w' rho w >= sum w_i^2 > 0` once the floor is applied, so there is no division by zero. A non-positive-definite rho is not a problem for the quadratic form.
- Weights must sum to 1. A rule with weight 0 is dropped before the FDM is computed.
- NaN handling: if any weighted rule is NaN on a day, the combined forecast is NaN for that day. It is never renormalised silently, so as not to change FDM implicitly. Instruments enter only when every weighted rule is defined.
- The FDM is a constant per run, estimated once on the research window (P3-11), not a rolling number. The charter fixes the estimation window.
- RECONCILED: long/flat is applied HERE, once, on the combined forecast: the ETF path calls `combine_forecasts(..., floor=0.0)` with signed per-rule inputs (P3-02/03 keep floor=-20). Flooring each rule first would give rules at -10 and +10 a long position, whereas combine-then-floor gives 0. Consequence stated in the charter: the average |combined| forecast of a long/flat book is about 5, not 10, so realised vol sits well below tau.
- Dropped speeds (cost filter, P3-11). The charter fixes GROUP weights (EWMAC group, breakout group). Within a group the weight is split equally across the rules that survive the per-instrument speed filter. `group_equal_weights(group_weights, surviving_by_group) -> dict[str, float]` implements this explicitly (never an implicit renormalisation inside `combine_forecasts`), and the FDM is computed per surviving-rule set.
- Source test: "avg |combined| about 10". With scalars chosen so that every rule has average |f| of 10, and the FDM applied, the combined average |f| should be about 10. The test uses correlated Gaussian fixtures. The tolerance is stated below.

## Tests & acceptance criteria (concrete test file names and assertions)
`tests/test_forecast_combine.py` (synthetic only):
- `test_fdm_hand_computed`: two rules with weights 0.5/0.5 and rho 0.5 give `w'rho w = 0.25 + 0.25 + 2*0.25*0.5 = 0.75`, so FDM = `1/sqrt(0.75)` = 1.1547 to 1e-12.
- `test_fdm_uncorrelated_two_rules`: rho = I and w = (0.5, 0.5) give sqrt(2).
- `test_fdm_perfect_correlation_is_one`.
- `test_fdm_cap`: many uncorrelated rules give FDM equal to 2.5, not above.
- `test_negative_correlation_floored`: rho off-diagonal -0.5 gives the same FDM as rho = I.
- `test_avg_abs_combined_about_10`: 6 rules with average |f| = 10 each and pairwise correlation about 0.6, multivariate normal, 50000 rows, give the combined mean |f| within 10 +/- 1.0 (source test).
- `test_combine_then_floor_not_floor_then_combine`: two rules at -10 and +10 with equal weights and FDM 1 give a combined forecast of 0 with `floor=0.0`, not a positive value (per-rule flooring would give 5).
- `test_group_equal_weights`: dropping one of six EWMAC speeds gives the five survivors an equal share of the EWMAC group weight, and the weights still sum to 1.
- `test_weights_validation`: negative weights or a sum != 1 raise.
- `test_nan_propagates`: a NaN in one weighted rule gives a NaN combined value, with no silent renormalisation.
- `test_pooled_correlation_uses_window_only`: appending rows after `end` does not change the matrix. A pairwise overlap below 250 raises.
- `test_no_weight_fitting_api`: `inspect.signature` of `combine_forecasts`, `fdm` has no returns/P&L parameter.
- `test_import_light` (subprocess): `firm.portfolio.forecast_combine` imports without `firm.live`, `firm.api`, `firm.runtime`. P0-06's isolation test, run against the full tree, still passes (new module is not imported by `firm.portfolio.__init__`).

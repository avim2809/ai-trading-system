## Rationale (cite source plan / literature)
Source plan P3-06 and Carver's position-sizing and buffering rules. Position = forecast-proportional risk budget, scaled by the instrument diversification multiplier (IDM) and target vol tau. Buffering stops trading until the position leaves a no-trade band, which cuts turnover and cost with little tracking loss. The ETF path is long/flat with gross at most 1.0x (design), so tau must be set ex ante from asset vols (OD-17), and the "gross-cap-bound" diagnostic exists to show how often the cap binds.

## Algorithm notes / pseudocode (keep the source plan's formulas exactly where they apply; correct them explicitly if wrong, with reason)
Source formulas, kept:
- `N = forecast * capital * IDM * weight * tau / (10 * multiplier * price * fx * sigma%)`
- `B = 0.1 * capital * IDM * weight * tau / (multiplier * price * fx * sigma%)`
- Trade only if the current position is outside `[N-B, N+B]`, trading to the nearest edge.
- `IDM = 1 / sqrt(w' H w)`, `H` = correlation of instrument sub-system returns, capped at 2.5.
- tau 12-15% for the source's futures setting (superseded by OD-17 for the ETF path).

Clarifications:
- Consistency check: `B` equals 0.1 times the position at forecast 10, that is, `N(f=10)`. This is Carver's "10% of the average position" buffer, and a test pins it.
- `sigma%` is the annualised vol as a fraction (P3-01's output). With `tau` as a fraction (0.10), the result is in units of the instrument (shares or contracts).
- `fx` converts instrument currency to the account currency (1.0 for USD ETFs).
- Long/flat: the COMBINED forecast arriving here is floored at 0 by P3-05 (per-rule forecasts stay signed upstream), so `N >= 0`. `buffered_trade` handles the lower edge at 0 (no short side because `target - B` can be negative, in which case the lower edge is clamped at 0).
- Rounding happens AFTER buffering, on the trade target, so that rounding cannot defeat the buffer. Futures round to the nearest integer contract. Fractional ETFs round to a small step, and non-fractional ETFs round to whole shares.
- Gross-cap diagnostic: `gross_cap_scale` returns whether any scaling was needed. P3-09 records the share of days where it is True, and P3-08 compares it to the 20% ceiling from OD-17 (a G-RESEARCH 6 robustness failure if exceeded).
- IDM: H is the matrix of instrument sub-system return correlations on the research window (P3-11). Off-diagonals are floored at 0 (same convention as FDM).

## Tests & acceptance criteria (concrete test file names and assertions)
`tests/test_sizing.py` (synthetic only):
- `test_target_position_hand_computed`: capital 100000, IDM 1.0, weight 0.1, tau 0.10, multiplier 1, price 100, fx 1, vol 0.20, forecast 10 gives `100000*0.1*0.10/(1*100*0.2)` = 50 shares. Forecast 20 gives 100. Forecast 0 gives 0.
- `test_buffer_is_10pct_of_full_position`: `buffer_width == 0.1 * full_position` to 1e-12.
- `test_buffered_trade_edges`: current inside the band stays unchanged. Current above `N+B` goes to `N+B`. Current below `N-B` goes to `N-B`. Lower edge clamped at 0 for the long/flat case.
- `test_turnover_falls_monotonically_with_buffer` (source test): on a fixed noisy random-walk forecast path, total traded quantity is strictly decreasing across buffer fractions {0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2}.
- `test_integer_rounding_futures`: multiplier 50 gives integer contracts, and rounding after buffering is verified.
- `test_etf_rounds_toward_zero`: a fractional ETF quantity is floored at the fractional step and a whole-share quantity is floored to an integer, matching `Allocator._qty_toward_zero` (checked against a literal table). `nearest` is used only when `multiplier > 1`.
- `test_fractional_etf_allowed`: `fractional=True` keeps a fractional quantity, and `False` gives whole shares.
- `test_idm_hand_computed_and_cap`: equal weights on 2 instruments with H offdiag 0.5 give `1/sqrt(0.75)` (for w = (0.5, 0.5)). 20 uncorrelated instruments give 2.5 (the cap).
- `test_gross_cap_scale_flag`: weights summing to 1.3 are scaled to 1.0 pro rata and flagged True. A sum of 0.8 is unchanged and flagged False.
- `test_zero_vol_or_price_raises`: non-positive price, vol or multiplier raises `ValueError`.
- `test_no_alpha_input`: signatures contain no returns/P&L argument.

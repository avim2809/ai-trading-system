## Algorithm notes / pseudocode (keep the source plan's formulas exactly where they apply; correct them explicitly if wrong, with reason)
Order (fixed in the prereg, no branching on results):
1. Load pre-seal ETF total-return and price series for the universe via `data_access`. Record `data_snapshot_id` (P2-02 manifest hashes).
2. Compute vol (P3-01) and the raw EWMAC and breakout series per instrument for every pre-registered speed or lookback.
3. Scalars: for each rule (six EWMAC speeds, five breakout lookbacks) compute one pooled scalar across instruments so that the pooled mean |raw*scalar| = 10 over the research window, measured on the UNCAPPED, UNFLOORED signed raw forecast (source P3-02 and P3-03; Carver convention). Never per-instrument. Per-rule forecasts stay signed; only the combined forecast is floored at 0.
4. Speed filter, PER INSTRUMENT: for each instrument and speed compute annualised turnover from the forecast (not from P&L), convert to a cost in Sharpe units using that instrument's P2-04 cost per trade and sigma, and drop speeds whose cost exceeds 1/3 of the charter's pre-registered expected rule Sharpe (source P3-02 rule: per instrument). This uses cost and turnover only. Per-instrument surviving-speed sets go into `constants.json`.
5. FDM: weights are the charter's fixed GROUP weights, split equally within a group across the rules that survive step 4 for that instrument (`group_equal_weights`, P3-05; pre-registered, no result-dependent choice). Build the pooled forecast correlation between the surviving SIGNED rules (not floored per rule), floor off-diagonals at 0, `FDM = 1/sqrt(w' rho w)`, cap 2.5 (source P3-05), computed per surviving-rule set.
6. IDM: run the sub-system (one instrument, unit risk) returns through the P3-09 engine at 1x cost, build correlation H pooled on the window, floor at 0, `IDM = 1/sqrt(w' H w)`, cap 2.5 (source P3-06). The weights come from P4-01 where available, otherwise the charter's equal or handcrafted weights. If P4-01 weights are not yet merged, the prereg names the charter weights explicitly, so there is no hidden dependency. `constants.json` stores a sha256 of the instrument-weight vector used; P3-08 step 1 verifies it equals the hash of the weights it sizes with, and fails otherwise.
7. Write `constants.json` containing scalars, surviving speeds, rho, FDM, H, IDM, window, `data_snapshot_id`, `gates.yaml` hash, code commit, seed. Compute sha256. Write `constants.md`.
8. Stop. The owner reviews, commits the addendum. Nothing is re-run after viewing; a re-run is a new trial and may require a new prereg.

Clarifications:
- Step 6 uses a backtest-like run, so it is a real trial in the ledger. Steps 3-5 are estimation trials and are recorded as `mode='registered'` rows with `config['kind']='constant_estimation'` that count toward N (OD-09 "do not round down").
- The estimation window is the full pre-seal research window defined in the prereg (an instrument contributes only after its 256-day gate). Full-window estimation is in-sample by construction, which is the accepted convention for scalars. It is consistent with the fact that the whole panel is already burned (OD-04). CPCV in P3-08 handles overfitting of the downstream choices, but cannot undo that scalars saw all of the pre-seal data. The report states this.

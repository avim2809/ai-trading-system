## Algorithm notes / pseudocode (keep the source plan's formulas exactly where they apply; correct them explicitly if wrong, with reason)
- Correlation: pairwise-complete, overlap >= 250 days (same rule as P1-03); fewer raises.
- Participation ratio `PR = (sum λ_i)^2 / sum λ_i^2` over correlation eigenvalues.
- ENB, gate form (Meucci 2009, weight-dependent): on the covariance `Sigma` of the asset-class P&L-contribution streams with unit weights over those streams (they already carry risk size), PCA `Sigma = E Lambda E'`, `p_i = (e_i' 1)^2 lambda_i / (1' Sigma 1)`, `ENB = exp(-sum p_i ln p_i)`, computed by calling P1-03's `firm.validation.diversification.effective_number_of_bets(Sigma, weights=np.ones(k))` (single implementation; no local re-implementation). This is the quantity the H4 gate uses, because correlation-eigenvalue entropy ignores risk size and can be passed by a book with 90% of risk in equities plus small uncorrelated legs. P0-08 freezes in `gates.yaml` the method name (`meucci_pca_pnl_cov`), the input level (asset-class P&L streams) and the thresholds, with an OD-16 register row (cross-file); `build_report` reads the method from `gates.yaml`, it is not chosen in this ticket.
- Diagnostics (reported, never gating): `enb_family` and `enb_asset_class_corr_entropy` = `exp(-sum p ln p)` with `p_i = lambda_i / sum lambda` over correlation eigenvalues (same as P1-03); participation ratio; diversification ratio; rolling mean pairwise correlation and rolling ENB (window from gates.yaml).
- Stress flag: any pair with `r_stress > 0.7` (threshold in `gates.yaml`) is listed in `stress_corr_flags` and printed prominently.
- MP edge: `λ_max = (1 + sqrt(N/T))^2` for N families, T overlap days, on standardized returns.
- Fisher z: as in the signature. Stress mask comes from `config/stress_periods.yaml` (P3-07) or a pre-registered volatility rule; take the mask as input, do not define it here.
- With 3-4 family streams `enb_family` is bounded by the family count, so the report prints the bound and marks family ENB uninformative if N_families < 3. The gate evaluates `enb_asset_class` only, as in the signed register.

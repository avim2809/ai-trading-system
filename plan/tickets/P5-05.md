## Rationale (cite source plan / literature)
Source P5-05: (a) take the multi-agent LLM pipeline out of the signal path, (b) anonymise entities if an LLM is ever used, (c) require a pinned model version and forward paper testing only, because an LLM cannot be backtested without look-ahead through its training data. Literature: Lopez-Lira & Tang (2023) and the subsequent look-ahead/memorisation critiques; Glasserman & Lin (2023) on anonymised headlines.

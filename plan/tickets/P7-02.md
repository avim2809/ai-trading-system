## Algorithm notes / pseudocode (keep the source plan's formulas exactly where they apply; correct them explicitly if wrong, with reason)
Per step (25%, then 50%, then 100% of the planned real allocation), G-LIVE-STEP requires:
- at least 3 months at the step;
- G-PAPER 2-4 hold on live fills (daily correlation at least 0.95 and annualised TE at most 25% of tau; realised cost at most 1.5x modelled; position-break and missed-review records);
- CUSUM monitor not alarmed (P5-03);
- drawdown within the charter envelope: `gates.yaml` key `g_live_step.dd_reference: survival_ref`, `dd_multiple: 1.0`, i.e. max DD <= 1.0 x max(p95, 2.5 tau) (the OD-16 split rule, soft tier; see the OD-16 register row). 1.5x is the G-DECOMMISSION trigger (P0-08), not a pass. Measured on a unitised time-weighted series (below);
- G-PAPER 3 on live fills needs at least 30 live fills across at least 10 instruments within the step; otherwise the step is extended, never passed. The approval file records fill count and instrument count;
- P3-10 forward-holdout check passed before step 1;
- owner sign-off committed in `research/approvals/`.
Daily fidelity and CUSUM monitoring run throughout, plus a monthly after-tax, after-cost report vs BM2 60/40 (P2-05 model), labelled live comparator, non-evidence. The G-DECOMMISSION combiner stays active (one trigger means PROBATION, two means weight 0 and approval needed to resume).

Capital flows and step mechanics:
- All G-LIVE-STEP and G-DECOMMISSION drawdown, HWM, CUSUM and TE computations run on a unitised, time-weighted return series. External deposits and withdrawals are recorded as durable events in `research/approvals/` and removed. Reason: the engine's kill switch tracks a dollar peak (`engine.py` L345 seeds `_peak_equity` from `initial_capital`; L1309-1310 raises it on `nav > peak`), so a step-up deposit would fake a new peak and erase drawdown, and a withdrawal would fake one (the 8/6 Alpaca incident, `config/live_alpaca.yaml` L352-356).
- Each step is implemented by funding the dedicated real account only to the step amount (preferred), or by a hard capital cap verified by a dry-run Allocator/engine test. `initial_capital` must equal funded equity at step start; any change goes through a signed config diff and a controlled restart. The immutable HWM series is rebased only by an owner-signed approval file.
- Before each step the owner runs a fault drill on the real-money instance with trading disarmed: soft and hard kill-switch tiers (P4-04), stale data, broker disconnect and the G-DECOMMISSION combiner (P5-01). The approval file records the result, `initial_capital` and the funded balance. The tiered kill switch and combiner must be enabled, not merely "active".
- Secrets and arming: `FIRM_ALLOW_TRADING=1` and live broker credentials/ports live only in a separate root-owned 0600 EnvironmentFile referenced only by the real-money unit, never in the shared `.env`. An owner pre-step checklist asserts that `systemctl show -p Environment,EnvironmentFiles` for ai-trading, ai-trading-alpaca, s2-forward-shadow and best-stocks-arm shows no `FIRM_ALLOW_TRADING` and no live keys or ports.
- Code source: the real-money instance runs from a separate checkout or worktree pinned to an owner-approved tag/commit with its own venv; its unit must not reference the research checkout or its `.venv`. Upgrades are a separate touches_live ticket with `live_import_smoke`, a full suite and a signed approval file. The step approval file records the deployed commit hash, and the step is void if that hash changes during the window without a new approval.
- The real-money account is distinct from the P6-03 paper account and is never reached by flipping the `:8002` paper unit's broker string in place.

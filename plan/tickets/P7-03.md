## Algorithm notes / pseudocode (keep the source plan's formulas exactly where they apply; correct them explicitly if wrong, with reason)
The review covers:
- Re-estimate vol, correlation, IDM and FDM on an expanding window with the charter's pre-registered procedure, with no performance optimisation. Post-seal data is read only through the owner-approved monitoring/ops access path (the same one the P5-04/P2-05 monthly reports use), never by disabling or bypassing `firm.research.seal`/`data_access`. This path is recorded as a HOLDOUT_POLICY exception for a family already unsealed by P3-10.
- Applying constants: re-estimates produced by the pre-registered expanding-window procedure on its pre-registered schedule are the same strategy, not a parameter change. They are applied only after owner sign-off, and each is logged as a ledger row (mode='registered', linked to the original prereg hash). Any change to the procedure, the constants list or the windows is an improvement and goes to IDEA. The report diffs old vs new constants and cites the prereg procedure hash.
- Review each charter (mechanism, tau, max-DD procedure, decommission rules).
- Reconcile tax against actual statements (Israeli rules, per P2-06 and the tax adviser's answer).
- Report the ledger N and DSR with the current N, including all counted unregistered and API-capture rows.
- Any proposed improvement re-enters the lifecycle at IDEA, as a new pre-registered trial.

## Tests & acceptance criteria (concrete test file names and assertions)
Report-only. Acceptance: the report cites ledger trial_ids for every number, states the N used, and is committed with the date from `date -u`.

## Definition of done
The annual report is committed and reviewed by the owner, and any follow-up ideas are logged as IDEA entries, not applied.

## Effort (agent-hours + human review minutes)
4 agent-hours; 120 review minutes.

## DO NOT
- Do not tune parameters on live or paper performance.
- Do not apply any re-estimated constant to the live instance without an owner decision and the ledger row above; anything outside the pre-registered procedure needs a new pre-registered trial and a separate touches_live ticket, and resets the G-LIVE-STEP clock.
- Do not apply improvements directly; they go through the lifecycle.
- Do not edit frozen preregistrations, charters or `gates.yaml` as part of the review.
- Do not leave out unregistered or captured runs from N.
